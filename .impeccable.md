## Design Context

### Users
Korean-speaking Christians using the AI chat app for Bible study, prayer guidance, and spiritual counsel. Context is personal, quiet, reflective — often used in moments of need or daily devotion.

### Brand Personality
**Reserved · Reverent · Refined.** The interface should feel like a well-bound book or a quiet study: dignified, unobtrusive, warm. No flash, no gimmicks. The brand is a trusted companion, not an entertainer.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Luxury/Refined — monochrome editorial.** Black & white with tinted neutrals. Space and silence are features. Typography does the heavy lifting. Reference: high-end print magazines, monastic simplicity, Japanese wabi-sabi.

### Typography
- **Headings**: Georgia, 'Times New Roman', serif — classic editorial authority
- **Body**: 'Noto Sans KR', 'Apple SD Gothic Neo', -apple-system, sans-serif
- **Scale**: 1.25 (major third) — fewer sizes, higher contrast
- **Measure**: max 65ch for body, generous leading

### Design Principles
1. **Space as luxury.** Generous whitespace is the primary design material.
2. **Typography first.** In a monochrome palette, type hierarchy IS the visual interest.
3. **No decoration without purpose.** Every line, border, and shadow must justify its existence.
4. **Quiet confidence.** The interface should be felt, not noticed.
5. **60-30-10 rule.** 60% white space, 30% text & structure, 10% accent.
